## Named gaps (hypotheses)

1. Brand apps (Convatec, Coloplast) help only their own customers; nothing brand-neutral helps a Singapore carer choose across brands.
2. Patient-facing wound apps are therapy-tied or clinician-gated; no consumer app combines dressing guidance with a local catalogue.
3. Marketplaces sell the same products with no guidance; EMIS's own Shopee and Lazada listings carry no education.

## False-corroboration check

- The "no consumer wound app with dressing guidance" claim rests on one search's results. It is **single-source**; confirm with an App Store and Play Store search in Singapore.
- PinkPharm's range is from a search summary only. **Unverified.**
